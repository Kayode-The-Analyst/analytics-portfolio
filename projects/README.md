# Research Projects

This section presents selected applied research and analytical projects developed across data analytics, predictive analytics, intelligent systems, customer analytics, energy analytics, and decision support.

Each project documents the problem being investigated, research motivation, objectives, methodology, data preparation, analytical approach, findings, limitations, and potential directions for further research.

The projects demonstrate the application of computing, data analytics, and emerging artificial intelligence techniques to real-world problems across areas including energy management, service operations, customer behaviour, and decision support.
